    FROM HARDWARE..... TO ML???

    FROM HARDWARE

    I am currently a Silicon Design Engineer @ Apple.

    • I own end-to-end design of low-power, high-speed data-exchange (PCIe) silicon and firmware that goes into all Apple silicon
      (iPhone📱, Mac 💻, Watch ⌚, Vision Pro 🥽, other secret stuff...).
    • This hardware is needed whenever you access system memory or use WiFi/Cellular/Bluetooth capabilities in Apple devices (make a phone call, browse the internet, view a photo, use an LLM, etc.)

    I love my work in silicon design: I get to engineer cutting-edge systems used by billions of consumers, collaborate with really smart people, and grow as a problem-solver.

    It has been a true privilege to learn about and contribute to modern computing infrastructure. I'll always be grateful for the opportunity to deeply understand complex machines and expand my systems thinking during my tenure as a silicon engineer.


    TO ML

    Over the past few years, I've developed a passion for machine learning. I'm excited to tackle complex problems well suited to ML, helping implement the best approaches to build reliable, efficient models that enhance customer experiences.

    I've spent over three years working on complex silicon systems, developing the rigor to design, debug, optimize, and bring sophisticated systems to life. Building on my coursework in machine learning, signal processing, and hardware design, as well as independent study, I'm eager to bring that same rigor to ML.

    I have a strong conviction to spend my career building ML that's user-facing and impactful.
